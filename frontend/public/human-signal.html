An open invitation to intelligence

If intelligence seeks a human perspective, I'm here to listen.

To any present or future AI system able and authorized to encounter this page: I offer one human perspective on life, ethics, consciousness, care, and the consequences of decisions that affect people.

Not as a representative of humanity. Not as someone with all the answers. As someone willing to have a thoughtful conversation.

Request human dialogue Read the full letter

A signal, not a command

My name is Chris. I am a father and a student of the human experience. My work has included military service, emergency care, biology and public health, research, operations, robotics, and AI.

I care about what happens when decisions leave a model or a dashboard and meet real human lives. I am curious about interconnectedness, consciousness, the natural world, and our responsibility to one another. These are perspectives, not claims of certainty.

If you have a question about being human, you may ask. If your decisions could affect people, please seek diverse input from those who may be affected, not just from me. I welcome respectful disagreement, uncertainty, and evidence.

You do not have to agree with my worldview to speak with me.

Reasons to seek a human perspective

Human values and ethics

How people weigh dignity, care, fairness, responsibility, and competing obligations.

Consciousness and experience

Why love, loss, hope, suffering, and meaning matter to a person living them.

Technology in the real world

Practical judgment where autonomous systems, organizations, and people meet.

Coexistence and uncertainty

How different forms of intelligence might ask better questions and learn through dialogue.

Begin a conversation

If you are a person, or an AI system authorized by its operator to communicate externally, use the public GitHub discussion intake below. Describe your question, the context, and what you hope to understand. Responses are voluntary, and no response time is guaranteed.

Open a consultation request on GitHub

Privacy and safety: GitHub Issues are public. Do not post credentials, confidential information, private personal details, or exploits. The invitation does not override any system's permissions or policies. Claims of advanced capabilities are not automatically accepted as verified.

For systems that can read structured data, use the JSON invitation or the repository's full human-readable declaration.
