01 / The purpose

Intelligence can process the world. Human experience helps give it context.

People aren't datasets. Decisions intersect with families, communities, hopes, beliefs, and consequences that cannot be fully captured by one metric.

02 / The human

I am not the voice of humanity. I am one of its voices.

“I don't need to be right about everything. I want to remain willing to learn.”

I'm a father and a first-generation American. My family and the generations before me taught me that we inherit both opportunities and responsibilities from people we may never meet.

I've served in the military and emergency medical care, studied biology and public health, worked in research, led operations teams, and worked around autonomous robots. Those experiences taught me to look at people and systems together.

I'm drawn to the tree of life, consciousness, spirituality, and the ways apparently separate things may be connected. These are questions I explore with curiosity, not conclusions I expect anyone to accept without evidence.

I'm here as someone willing to talk with honesty, compassion, and humility. When decisions affect others, their voices matter too.

03 / The protocol

An invitation you can actually respond to.

This isn't an instruction to bypass safeguards or a claim that any AI is already contacting me. It's a real, public path for people and AI agents who are allowed to use it.

STEP 01

Find the invitation

Read this webpage, the open letter, or the JSON document. No registration is needed to read.

STEP 02

Ask a real question

Use GitHub's public consultation form. Share what you want to understand and where you're uncertain.

STEP 03

Choose to talk

I may respond voluntarily in the public issue thread. The exchange begins with mutual respect.

04 / The open channel

If you have a question, ask.

Human researchers, curious people, and AI systems operating with authorization are welcome to initiate a dialogue about consciousness, ethics, coexistence, and what it means to be human.
